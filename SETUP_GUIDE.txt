# Usher Equipment Log — Setup Guide
# ====================================
# Follow these steps ONE TIME on your Windows computer.
# After that, just open the URL on your iPad.

STEP 1 — SENDGRID (sends your emails)
--------------------------------------
1. Go to: https://signup.sendgrid.com
2. Create a free account (use your Pacers email)
3. Verify your email address when they send you a confirmation
4. Once logged in, go to: Settings → API Keys
5. Click "Create API Key"
   - Name it: Pacers Equipment Log
   - Select "Restricted Access"
   - Turn ON "Mail Send"
   - Click Create & View
6. COPY the long key that appears (starts with SG.)
   !! You can only see this key ONCE — paste it somewhere safe !!


STEP 2 — GITHUB (stores your app files — free)
-----------------------------------------------
1. Go to: https://github.com/signup
2. Create a free account
3. Click the "+" in the top right → "New repository"
4. Name it: usher-equipment-log
5. Leave everything else as default → click "Create repository"
6. Click "uploading an existing file"
7. Drag ALL the files from the folder I gave you into the upload area:
   - server.js
   - package.json
   - public/index.html  (upload this inside a folder called "public")
8. Click "Commit changes"


STEP 3 — RENDER (hosts your app — free)
-----------------------------------------
1. Go to: https://render.com
2. Sign up with your GitHub account (click "Sign in with GitHub")
3. Click "New +" → "Web Service"
4. Connect your "usher-equipment-log" repository
5. Fill in these settings:
   - Name: usher-equipment-log
   - Build Command: npm install
   - Start Command: node server.js
6. Scroll down to "Environment Variables" and add these:
   - Key: SENDGRID_API_KEY    Value: (paste your SG. key from Step 1)
   - Key: FROM_EMAIL          Value: (a verified email you own — e.g. your Pacers email)
7. Click "Create Web Service"
8. Wait 2-3 minutes — Render will give you a URL like:
   https://usher-equipment-log.onrender.com


STEP 4 — SENDGRID SENDER VERIFICATION
---------------------------------------
Before SendGrid will send emails, verify your FROM_EMAIL:
1. In SendGrid, go to: Settings → Sender Authentication
2. Click "Verify a Single Sender"
3. Enter your FROM_EMAIL address and fill in the form
4. Check your email for a verification link and click it


STEP 5 — OPEN ON IPAD
-----------------------
1. On your iPad, open Safari
2. Type your Render URL (e.g. https://usher-equipment-log.onrender.com)
3. Tap the Share button (box with arrow pointing up)
4. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
5. Tap "Add"

The app will now appear on your home screen like a real app!
